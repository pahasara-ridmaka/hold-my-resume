Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account or managing applications, including:

  • Account credentials (name, email address, password hash).
  • Job tracking records (job titles, company names, URLs).
  • Uploaded documents (resumes and cover letter files).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to maintain and deliver your application pipeline, compute performance metrics in your analytics dashboard, and safeguard account security.

3. Data Storage & Security

We implement strict security measures to protect your documents and application records against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information, resumes, or job search data to third parties or recruiters.

5. Account Deletion & Rights

You have full control over your data. You may update your profile information or delete specific job applications and attached documents at any time.
